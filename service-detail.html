About This Service

Our Exterior Wash & Shine service goes far beyond a standard car wash. We start with a thorough pre-rinse to loosen dirt, followed by a meticulous hand wash using pH-neutral soap. A clay bar decontamination removes embedded fallout and bonded contaminants, restoring silky-smooth paint. We then apply a premium carnauba wax or synthetic sealant for months of durable protection and deep gloss — leaving your vehicle looking better than the day you drove it off the lot.

Suitable for daily drivers and show vehicles alike. All products are eco-friendly, biodegradable, and safe for all paint types including wraps and ceramic coatings.

Our Process

Pre-Rinse & Foam

2

Hand Wash

Two-bucket wash method with plush microfiber mitts to prevent swirl marks.

3

Clay Bar

Wax & Shine

Premium wax or sealant application and final detailing for a showroom finish.
