About Wash & Go

Born from a simple idea: exceptional cleaning should come to you, not the other way around.

Who We Are

Cleaning Made Convenient, Results Made Perfect

Founded in 2019, Wash & Go set out to transform the way people think about car and home cleaning. We believed that premium results and genuine convenience could coexist — and we built a business to prove it.

Today, our team of fully trained, insured technicians serves thousands of households and businesses across the region — bringing showroom-quality cleaning directly to your doorstep using only eco-certified products.

Our Journey

How We Got Here

From a single van and a big dream to a fully equipped team serving thousands — here's our story so far.

2019

Founded with One Van

Marcus and Priya started Wash & Go from their driveway with one van, two pressure washers and an unwavering belief in mobile convenience.

2020

Expanded to Home Cleaning

Customer demand led us to launch our home cleaning division, allowing us to serve households comprehensively under one brand.

2022

500th Five-Star Review

Reached the milestone of 500 five-star reviews across Google and Trustpilot. A moment we're incredibly proud of.

2023

Eco Certification Achieved

Became fully certified by the Green Cleaning Association, formalising our commitment to environmental responsibility.

2024

Fleet & Commercial Launch

Launched dedicated B2B contracts for fleet operators and commercial premises, growing our team to 12 technicians.
