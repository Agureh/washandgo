Our Story

Built on a Belief That Clean Should Be Convenient

Wash&Go was founded in 2016 by Marcus Chen, a certified automotive detailer who grew frustrated watching customers wait hours at traditional car washes for mediocre results. Marcus believed there was a better way — mobile, professional, and guaranteed to impress.

What began as a one-man operation with a single truck has grown into a team of 20+ certified professionals serving thousands of happy customers each year — now including premium house cleaning services alongside our signature car detailing.

Our guiding philosophy has never changed: show up on time, do extraordinary work, and leave every customer raving.
