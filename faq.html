Car Detailing

A standard exterior wash will not remove deep scratches, but our Paint Correction service uses machine polishing compounds to significantly reduce or eliminate swirl marks and light-to-moderate scratches. Deep gouges that have penetrated the clear coat require panel repainting, which we do not offer. We'll assess your paint during booking and recommend the appropriate service.
We recommend:
  • Exterior wash: Every 2–4 weeks
  • Interior clean: Every 4–8 weeks
  • Full detail: 2–4 times per year
  • Ceramic coating: Once every 2–3 years
Frequency depends on usage, environment, and your standards. Our recurring plans make staying on schedule easy and affordable.
Ceramic coating is a liquid polymer that chemically bonds to your vehicle's paint, creating a protective layer that repels water, dirt, UV rays, chemicals, and minor scratches. Benefits include: easier washing, longer-lasting shine, protection from oxidation, and reduced waxing frequency. For most vehicle owners who care about paint longevity, it's absolutely worth the investment. We offer a warranty certificate with all ceramic coating applications.

A Standard Clean covers the visible surfaces — vacuuming, mopping, wiping down surfaces, bathroom and kitchen cleaning. A Deep Clean goes further: inside appliances, grout scrubbing, baseboards, behind furniture, window sills, and all the areas typically missed in routine cleaning. We recommend starting with a Deep Clean if it's your first time with us, then switching to Standard for maintenance.
